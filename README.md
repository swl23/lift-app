# Lift App

A lean, flexible, phone-friendly web app for running a lifting program. Design a
split, let the app track which day is next, tap off sets at the gym, and see
your progress over time.

## Why

Most lifting apps lock you into a program, whether it's StrongLifts 5x5 or
Starting Strength. The app exists only to support the program and, often, some
dogmatic philosophy of weightlifting or exercise to go along with it. These apps
limit your ability to do anything off-plan or create your own split. My app lets
you create your own program. It also makes it easy for you to log one-off lifts
without disrupting your current split.

I'm also using the process of building this app as an opportunity to practice
system design, React, and building a real multi-user app end to end.
